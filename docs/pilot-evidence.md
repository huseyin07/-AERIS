# Genuine pilot and receipt evidence

No users, customer payments or pilot results are invented for this release. The owner excluded a live technical payment test. Funding a wallet does not constitute payment volume or business usage.

## Record only an actual use

| Field | Evidence to retain privately | Public application excerpt |
|---|---|---|
| Business task | Actual vendor/service and invoice purpose | Consented, anonymized use case |
| Participant | Actual participant and date; consent for quotes | Count only confirmed participants |
| Workflow result | Observed actions, confusion and errors | Exact outcome and limitations |
| Payment | Authorized recipient, amount and final canonical receipt | Public hash only with the owner's consent |
| Circle role | Actual wallet identity and signing path | Distinguish user-controlled wallet from developer treasury |
| Feedback | Original comments and permission | Short approved quote; no invented endorsement |

## Feedback questions

1. What USDC payment or investigation task did you need to complete?
2. Could you find the intended action without assistance? Where did you hesitate?
3. Did the recipient, amount and maximum fee make the approval understandable?
4. Could you recover your history and independently check the outcome?
5. What prevented you from using this for an actual business task?

## Receipt acceptance

A hash alone is insufficient. AERIS checks exact sender, recipient, six-decimal amount, zero native value, transfer calldata, matching USDC Transfer log, successful receipt, canonical block and at least another block of confirmation. The Circle treasury path additionally verifies Circle wallet identity. Personal wallet receipts do not automatically establish Circle identity. A cancellation consumes the original nonce and is never counted as payment volume. Keep the raw receipt/evidence export and exact release commit.

Do not submit a technical self-test as business traction. Obtain real usage independently, then update the submission's currently unsubstantiated metrics.
